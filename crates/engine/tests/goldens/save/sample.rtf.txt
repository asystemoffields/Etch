\pard\plain\s7\ql\sa80\fs56 The Open Studio Handbook\par
\pard\plain\ql How a small community of makers shares space, tools and time\par
\pard\plain\ql Every Thursday evening the old print shop on Alder Street fills with people who make things. {\b Painters} set up by the north windows, {\i letterpress} regulars claim the back room, and somebody always brings bread. This handbook collects what we have learned about running a shared studio: the routines that keep it pleasant, the tools we pool, and the small agreements that let thirty strangers become a crew.\par
\pard\plain\s1\outlinelevel0\ql\sb240\sa80\keepn\b\fs40 Why share a studio?\par
\pard\plain\ql Rent is the obvious reason, but it is not the best one. A shared room puts your work next to work you would never have seen otherwise. A ceramicist explains glaze chemistry to a comic artist; a photographer borrows a weaver's loom for a still life. The cross-talk is the point.\par
\pard\plain\s8\ql\sb240\li720\ri720\i A studio is a place where unfinished things are welcome.\par
\pard\plain\s2\outlinelevel1\ql\sb240\sa80\keepn\b\fs32 What we agree on\par
\pard\plain\ql\ls1\ilvl0\fi-360\li720 {\listtext\pard\plain \u8226?\tab}{\b Clean as you go. }Leave a table the way you would like to find it.\par
\pard\plain\ql\ls1\ilvl0\fi-360\li720 {\listtext\pard\plain \u8226?\tab}{\b Label what is yours. }Unlabelled supplies on the commons shelf are fair game.\par
\pard\plain\ql\ls1\ilvl0\fi-360\li720 {\listtext\pard\plain \u8226?\tab}{\b Ask before you borrow. }Even from the shelf, a quick note helps everyone keep track.\par
\pard\plain\ql\ls1\ilvl0\fi-360\li720 {\listtext\pard\plain \u8226?\tab}{\b Quiet hours }run from 9 to 11 on weekday mornings.\par
